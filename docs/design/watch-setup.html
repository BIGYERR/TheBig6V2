Connect your watch

The setup a runner walks through once, so a run recorded on the watch fills its run card by itself (D292). It lives in a Your watch block on the Bugs page, which becomes the Profile page, and a one-time card on This Week leads there the first time. Steps is a guide, one screen at a time, for the first setup. Checklist is one page whose rows tick themselves: what Your watch opens every time after. The app reads Health first, then asks the athlete to set up the watch's own app, so it can check that step the moment they come back.
