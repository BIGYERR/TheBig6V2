The anchor nudge, played on your own two runs

What we ruled, built in v0.256.0

D273. A leg day one or two days before a long run of 75 minutes or more keeps the squat as written and the circuit, keeps every hip and ankle drill, and drops the glute block, the eccentric calf block and the carry. The day does not move.

What we are adding, proposed

The nudge. Two easy runs in a row (recovery or long run cards, never a speed session), each more than a chart row off the prescribed pace, faster or slower. After the second one is logged, a sheet says what your runs imply your mile is and offers the pencil with that number filled in. The app never changes the number itself. One run never speaks. Skipped runs do not count. Silent in the last three weeks, when the mile is locked.

  • Your anchor is an 8:00 mile, entered in week 5. Recovery 10:30, tempo 9:25, 10K 9:00.
  • 31 October: 5.46 mi in 60:10, 11:01 a mile, 31 s slower than the plan. 7 November: 5.52 mi in 60:59, 11:03, 33 s slower. A chart row on your card is about 30 s. Both runs qualify; the second one opens the sheet.
  • The number offered is the slower of the two runs read on the recovery column (8:33), rounded to the slower row: 9:00. Your tempo on 26 October (10:23 a mile) reads 8:54 and agrees.
  • Keep 8:00 and it stays quiet until two new runs qualify. From week 12 the mile is locked and the sheet never shows.
