The anchor nudge, three places it could live

Your case, as the app saw it: anchor 10:30 mile, Saturday prescribed 13:20/mi, logged 11:03/mi, the second easy run in a row more than a chart row faster. The app never rewrites the anchor. It offers the pencil once, and only while the pencil is unlocked.

  • Fires only on recovery and long run cards, never on a speed session, and only after two in a row each a chart row (about 35 s/mi) or more faster than prescribed.
  • The number offered is the slower of the two runs, rounded to the slower row. Your 11:01 and 11:03 read as 8:31 and 8:33 by the recovery column; the tempo column says 8:54; the offer says 9:00.
  • Silent in the last three weeks, when the pencil is locked. For you that is week 12 on. This week is the last week it could speak.
  • Dismissed once, it stays quiet until two new runs qualify.
