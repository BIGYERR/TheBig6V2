Measured on today's build: across 891 programs and 61,893 training days, no round or block holds more than three moves. The five-move cool down is the tallest card a program makes. The five-move Wildcard round is here as a stress test; Wildcards open on their own screen today, not in the deck.

What the After card changes

  • One line under the title gives the rounds or sets and the rest. The label above the title (Round, Prehab, Main, Core, After the run), the two chips and the sentence that repeated them are gone.
  • Each move gets one prescription line, "10 each side · RPE 7". Today it says it twice: "8–12 each @ RPE 7", then "Sets 2 × 10 /side as prescribed".
  • The labels Sets, Load and "as prescribed" are gone. The weight box carries lbs.
  • Log sits at the end of the weight row, not a full width bar under every move.
  • Opening a move's sets never makes the card scroll: the other moves fold to make room. The table's last row measures every move of every card opened.
  • No dashes in what you read. "Leg circuit — runner armor" reads "Leg circuit". Ranges read "60 to 90 sec".

What you picked

  • In place, others fold. The move you tap opens where it is and the other moves shrink to one line each. Tap a folded move to open it instead. Tap the arrow again to close.
  • Past four sets a move shows one set at a time behind numbered buttons, as the app does today, so a five set move still fits.
  • Auto is Roomy text. A card of four or more moves (only Wildcards have one) keeps the move you are on open and folds the rest to one line; tap one to open it. A short phone like the SE steps down to Tight.
