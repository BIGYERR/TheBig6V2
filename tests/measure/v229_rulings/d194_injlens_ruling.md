## The rules

**R1 (new, D194). The plan that governs a card is the plan of the day the card is on.** Outside `buildProgram`, every reader of the athlete's injury reads it from the day: the day's `_ovKey` patch (`dayOverlayInfo(day).patch.injury`), and only when the day carries no stamp, `prog.cfg.injury` (which on an app program is nothing). The precedent is already in the file: V175 D27 keyed the swap universe on the day's stamp (`swapUniverseFor` :9977, "THE UNIT IS THE DAY"), and `applyOverlays` builds the variant from the same patch. One helper, reading the file's own carrier; no constant. A day before the injury's `from`, or after its `to`, has no stamp and trains under no plan, which is what the athlete's calendar says. Bridge and halfstep patches ride through unchanged and `injuryPlan` decides (halfstep caps and drops nothing, :7962). For V229 the lens carries the injury only, not the whole patch: the day's equipment on a travel day is a second, unmeasured differential and is not this ruling's.
