## D221 — P-REOPENSTAMP: on a dose=dist form the planned-miles stamp is derived. It is re-derived on every save and never read back into the wheel. A reopened long run clears in one gesture.

**Finding.** The V148 contract is "the third derives": on a dose=dist form the athlete logs time, miles is the prescription unless he overrides it, pace derives, and `persistLogFields` :14823 writes the planned miles into `run_dist` so the twenty readers have one surface. D215 Amendment 1 named the defect and parked it; D218 moved when the stamp lands and left the defect whole (mB finding 2). Mario's long runs are all dose=dist. This is the ruling the amendment deferred.

**The rule.** A stored `run_dist` that is byte-equal to the stamp the day's own dose would write, `String(dose.mi)`, is the stamp, and the stamp is derived: the reopen does not seed the miles wheel from it, and the rebuild re-derives it from what the form holds. One edit, at the one site that reads a stored `run_dist` back onto a wheel that carries a plan, `cardioFieldHTML` :13814: the hidden value passed to `iaWheelHTML('dist','log_run_dist', …, null, dose.mi)` is '' when `e.run_dist === String(dose.mi)`, else `e.run_dist` as today. The predicate reads the same object the stamp was written from (`doseDerived` returns `dose.mi`; :14823 stores `String(_dd.dist)`), so it round-trips by construction and no constant is invented. The other three miles-wheel sites (:13810 time form, :13825 `reps_time`, :13834 generic) carry no plan and never stamp miles; they seed as today.

**Why bytes and not a mark, and not numbers.** Three sites were on the table.
- A mark in the entry (`run_dist` plus a flag) is the semantically clean model, and it is the one I would choose on a fresh store. It is a stored-format change (cross-cutting), it must ride `parked` through `setCardioSwap` or the mark is lost on a swap, and it does nothing for the stamps already on Mario's phone, every one of which is unmarked. Rejected for this build.
- Dropping a stamp-equal value in the rebuild when minutes are zero fails the Log tap: an athlete who rolls miles to the plan and taps Log with no time passes the empty test on "3.10", `persistLogFields` writes it, and the drop would empty the entry under a `Run logged ✓` toast. Rejected.
- Numeric equality (`+e.run_dist === +dose.mi`) would treat an athlete's rolled "3.10" as the plan too. On a dose=dist form a rolled plan-equal value does carry no information beyond "as planned", but the athlete rolled it and tapped Log, and the form said it logged; dropping it on reopen breaks the same promise. Rejected.
- Bytes. The wheel writes dec3 ("3.10", "4.00", "13.10"); the stamp writes `String(dose.mi)` ("3.1", "4", "13.1"). They differ on every plan with zero or one decimal: 495 of 511 lattice dose=dist days, and 15 of 15 on HALF_MANNY (printed above). They collide on two-decimal plans, 16 of 511 in the lattice, 0 of 15 on Mario's program, and on V148 to V231 entries whose miles were typed in the number box exactly as the plan reads. The cost in the collision case is named below and accepted.

**The athlete-rolled-miles case, decided.**
- Rolled off the plan (any value whose bytes differ from the stamp: "3.10", "4.20", "0.75", a typed legacy "5"): seeded as today, carried as an athlete value, survives a time clear as a miles-only log (the athlete's own claim; `run_mins` '' and `run_pace` '' as today, 511/511 same shape, mB finding 7).
