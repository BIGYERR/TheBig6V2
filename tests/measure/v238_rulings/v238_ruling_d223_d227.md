## D223 P-RESTMOVELOG — a rest-day jog is its own record on the day; the moved-in session starts empty

**Finding (m1, V237).** The rest sheet writes the jog into the SAME keys the session form rebuilds: `rest_cardio`/`rest_type`/`rest_mins`, the shared `rpe`, and a sum into `run_dist`/`bike_mins`/`swim_yards`. After "Training anyway?" moves a session onto that day, the first rebuild drops the three `rest_*` keys 20,160/20,160 (Done) and 20,160/20,160 (RPE); the jog's sport key is erased on 6,912/20,160 (every lift and reps_dist form, every cross-sport form); on a same-sport form the jog's number opens as the session's own (13,248/13,500 seeded, `Logged ✓` 13,500/13,500, nudge 20,160/20,160) and entering the real session value replaces it (5,760/5,760 run, 3,600/3,600 bike, 4,140/4,140 swim: week MILES reads 5.2 where the oracle is 8.9). The jog's RPE 7 becomes the session's RPE on 20,160/20,160 Done-untouched. The only reader of `rest_*` (the rest hero line) stops rendering at the move, so from the move on the minutes exist nowhere the athlete can see, and the rebuild deletes them.

**What a moved-in session over a logged jog IS.** One day, two efforts. It is a double day, not a brick (a brick is two sports back to back as one session; this is a logged easy effort followed by the week's prescribed session, hours apart or not). The jog is bonus work with no prescription behind it: the log is the only record it has, so the log keeps everything the sheet took (minutes, distance, RPE). The session is the prescription that was moved: it opens exactly as it would on its own day (prescription owns the fixed dimension, the log captures the free one), and nothing the jog holds may stand in for a number the athlete has not entered. D213 applies unchanged: "the log is the athlete's record of what the watch showed, and a watch number cannot be retyped from memory a week later." A move is a scheduling gesture; it erases nothing. D213 Amendment 1's reason for parking this ("carrying `rest_*` would keep a flag no screen shows while the jog's miles still vanish through the shared `run_dist`") was right about the flat keys: the fix is not to carry them, it is to give the jog one home that no session reader reads.

**What changes.**
1. **Shape.** The `ia_logs_` day entry gains one key, `restLog`, an object keyed by sport in the shape `parked` already uses: `restLog = { run:{mins:37, dist:3.7, rpe:7}, bike:{mins, dist?, rpe}, swim:{mins, dist, rpe}, walk:{mins, dist?, rpe}, row:{mins, rpe} }`. Numbers, as today's writer stores them. Units are the sheet's: miles for run, walk and bike, yards for swim. `dist` is stored when the sheet took one (today the bike sheet asks for a distance and drops it; the record keeps it, no reader needs it).
2. **One writer.** `applyRestCardio`'s merge (:1434-:1444) writes `restLog[type]` and stamps `week` and `ts` like every other `ia_logs_` writer. It no longer writes `rest_cardio`, `rest_type`, `rest_mins`, it no longer writes `rpe`, and it no longer sums into `run_dist`/`bike_mins`/`swim_yards`. Those seven top-level keys are the session's and nobody else's.
3. **Carry.** `persistLogFields` carries `restLog` through the rebuild exactly as it carries `sets` and `parked` (:14812-:14814, :14828-:14829). `setCardioSwap` parks and restores sport keys only; `restLog` rides through untouched because it is not in `CARDIO_PARK_FIELDS`. `writeSetDraft`/`clearSetDraft` are read-modify-write and keep it (m2).
4. **The seed is blind by construction.** `buildLogHTML`'s slider seed (`e.rpe`), `cardioFieldHTML`'s wheel and box seeds, `cardioEntryLive`, `_hasLog` and the D221 stamp test all read top-level keys. With the jog out of those keys, nothing seeds, the card opens DRAFT (`Log`), the nudge is off, the slider reads `Move slider`, and a Done-untouched stores `rpe ''` (D219). No change to any of those readers.
5. **Legacy (question 6).** Entries already on the phone in the V237 shape (`rest_cardio:true` + `rest_type` + `rest_mins` + `rpe` + summed sport keys) are read through one pure lens, `restNorm(e)`: if `e.restLog` exists, `e` as is; else if `e.rest_cardio`, the V238 shape derived from the entry alone (`restLog[rest_type] = {mins:rest_mins, rpe:rpe}`; `run_dist>0` → `restLog.run.dist`; `swim_yards>0` → `restLog.swim.dist`; `bike_mins>0` → `restLog.bike.mins`, the sum winning over `rest_mins` because it is the fuller record), with the flat keys, `rpe` and the three sport keys cleared (on a V237 rest entry those keys can only be the jog's: no session ever wrote there, and a rebuild would already have dropped `rest_cardio`). Every reader this ruling names reads through the lens, and every writer persists what it read, so a legacy entry converts on the athlete's next save and never on boot (session call 8, the §12 legacy stamped-only line). Siting is the session's: I recommend the lens sits in `getLogs`/`getLogsFor`, the one read of `ia_logs_`, so no per-site latch exists anywhere; the write-back then happens on the next `saveLogs` of any entry, which is a gesture, not a boot. Legacy moved-in days already rebuilt on V237 (`{rpe:7, run_dist:"3.7"}`) are indistinguishable from a session and are left as one, credited once. A legacy cross-type "Log more" (`rest_type:"bike", run_dist:3.7`, run minutes already gone) lenses to `{bike:{mins:20,rpe:5}, run:{dist:3.7}}`; the lens invents nothing, and a type with no `mins` gets no hero line.

**What deliberately does NOT change.** `applyRestMove`'s data part (it reads no log and still should not: the move is a calendar write). `applyRestDayMoves`, `restDayEligible`, `openDayKey`. The seven session keys and their meaning. `parked` and D213. `plannedVsLogged`'s pairing (below). The sheet's inputs, chips, ceiling (`max="600"`), swim reuse of the distance box, its RPE chips and their 5 default, its copy: all P-RESTWHEEL. `buildProgram`: no program output moves.

## D224 — readers: sum readers add the jog, pair readers ignore it, max readers take it as its own candidate
