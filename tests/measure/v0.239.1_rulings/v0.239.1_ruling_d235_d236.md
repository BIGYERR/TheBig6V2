## D235 P-TIMERDRAG: a tap never strands the floating rest timer, and a cancelled drag snaps to an edge

Evidence (handoff §12, Post-V239 semver pass, reproduced at 375 px): `_rtWireDrag`'s pointermove writes `top` and `left`
on every move, below its 5 px threshold too, so a tap with finger jitter strands the timer mid-screen (card from x=179
to x=99, the tab left at x=99 after minimising). `pointercancel` only clears `dragging`, so a cancelled drag is left
where the finger was (x=230), never snapped to an edge.

What changes (index.html, `_rtWireDrag`):
1. pointermove: after the threshold line (`if(Math.abs(dx)>5||Math.abs(dy)>5)moved=true;`), `if(!moved)return;`. Below
   the threshold nothing is written: neither `top` nor `left`.
2. pointercancel snaps like pointerup: it ends the drag, restores the snap transition, and when `moved` it docks the
   timer to the nearer edge exactly as pointerup does (`_rtSide` from the centre, `anchor.style.left=''`,
   `_rtApplySide()`). A cancel never runs pointerup's tap actions (no expand, no collapse).

What deliberately does not change: the 5 px threshold, pointerup's tap behaviour (tab tap expands, card tap collapses
during the intro), the touchmove guard, `_rtApplySide`, and every other timer path.

## D236 P-SEMVERLABEL: the app shows its version in semver everywhere

Evidence: index.html:18105 sets the bug-report page's label to `'BUILD v'+IA_VERSION` and index.html:18072 writes
`'build: v'+IA_VERSION` into the bug-report context, where `IA_VERSION` is the raw meta. With the meta at `239` both read
`v239`, a bare integer (Mario, three times: the debug page must reflect the semver system).

What changes (index.html):
1. One helper prints the version as people read it: `v` plus the canonical `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`. A pre-semver integer N
   reads `0.N.0` (239 prints `v0.239.0`); a meta written with its own leading v or V keeps exactly one v (never `vv`).
   Anything else prints as given after one `v`, so the label never throws.
2. Both labels use it: `BUILD v0.239.1` on the bug-report page and `build: v0.239.1` in the report context.
3. The meta becomes `<meta name="ia-version" content="0.239.1">`, the last replacement of the build, and its comment
   (today "Bump this one number every release.") says the version is `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`, picked by Mario every release.

What deliberately does not change: the update check (`remote !== IA_VERSION`: an installed 239 sees `0.239.1` and shows
the update banner, which is right); `IA_VERSION` itself (still the raw meta, which the update check compares); every
other string.
